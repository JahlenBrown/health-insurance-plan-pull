## Document Conflict

Narrower than the first pass suggested. The website's "30-day supply" claim matches BD's Schedule of Benefits, which the PDF confirms verbatim: *"(30-day supply†)"* with a footnote limiting certain drugs to 30 days. SMB's headline says "up to a 60-day formulary supply," but SMB's own footnote 5 admits *"Certain medications limited to 30-day supply"* — so the two documents may not flatly disagree once you read past SMB's summary table. Still worth a human resolving with HUSHP Member Services (per the plan profile's own `open_items`), but not the stark 30-vs-60 contradiction it first appeared to be.

## Confirmed Mismatch

The vision-care page's routine/non-routine framing is now **directly contradicted by the primary document text**, not just the plan profile's citation: BD SoB p.7 states the $35 copayment applies to the *"Routine vision exam[]"* and there is no "non-routine vision exam" line item anywhere in the Schedule of Benefits. The website's page appears to simply have this backwards. Severity: critical — this is the plan's own documented "not everything preventive is free" trap, and the live site is currently telling members the opposite of what it should.

## Needs New Slot

All 5 facts from the first pass remain unregistered, but 3 now carry direct document citations rather than being website-only claims:

1. **`rx_pbm_subcontractor`** — still genuinely undocumented. Read the full BD pharmacy section (SoB p.6, body pp.51-54) directly: no PBM is named anywhere, only "Blue Cross and Blue Shield." Confirms the plan profile's own caveat; the website (CVS Caremark) may be the only public source naming it.
2. **`rx_antimalarial_cost_share`** — now document-confirmed: BD SoB p.6, "you will pay 50% (instead of your applicable copayment)."
3. **`rx_contraceptive_waiver_scope`** — reclassified. Initially looked like the website might be overstating the waiver; BD p.53 shows the website is actually accurate (brand-name waived too, conditional on medical necessity — a nuance the site omits but doesn't contradict).
4. **`mental_health_visit_limit`** — document is silent on an overall cap (doesn't contradict the website's "no limit" claim, doesn't affirm it either).
5. **`vision_eyewear_coverage`** — now an exact document match (BD SoB p.7: pediatric 35%/55% coinsurance, adult not covered).
