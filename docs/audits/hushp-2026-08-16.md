## Document Conflicts

The plan profile already flags `rx_retail_supply_limit_CONFLICT`: the Schedule of Benefits says retail Rx is a 30-day supply, but the AY2026-27 Summary of Medical Benefits says 60-day. **The live website sides with the Schedule of Benefits**: it states "Copayments for prescriptions purchased at a retail pharmacy are up to a 30-day supply" verbatim. This corroborates BD as the operative figure and sharpens the existing finding — the Summary (SMB p.6) is the outlier document, not a third, independent answer.

- Citation: BD SoB p.6 (30-day, matches website) vs SMB p.6 (60-day, conflicts with both)

## Mismatch — flagged for review

**`preventive_care_cost_share` (routine vision sub-fact)** — the plan profile's known audit trap is that routine vision exams are *not* free: "$35 copayment per visit in-network" (BD SoB p.6, footnote SoB p.9), specifically called out because members expect all routine/preventive care to be $0.

The live Vision Care page inverts which exam type carries the copay:
- "one routine vision exam per plan year" — **no copay mentioned**
- "Non-Routine Vision Exams" — **"$35 copayment"** in-network, 30% coinsurance out-of-network

Either the website has the routine/non-routine labels swapped relative to the governing document, or the AY2026-27 benefit design changed the routine exam to $0 and the profile's citation is now stale (it was extracted from the 8-1-2026 Benefit Description; the vision page may reflect a later update). This is exactly the class of finding this tool exists to surface and should not be resolved by picking a side — a human needs to open BD SoB p.6/p.9 and the current vision page side by side.

- Verdict: **mismatch**, severity high (this is the plan's own documented "preventive isn't all free" trap; if the website now contradicts it, that's member-facing, not just an internal inconsistency)
- Citation: BD SoB p.6, footnote SoB p.9 vs https://hushp.harvard.edu/benefits/student-health-insurance-plan-ship/vision-care/
